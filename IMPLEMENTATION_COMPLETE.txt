❓ COMMON QUESTIONS
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Q: Does it cost anything?
A: No! Google Ads API is completely free. No per-request charges.

Q: What credentials do I need?
A: 5 pieces: Client ID, Client Secret, Developer Token, Refresh Token, Customer ID
   (See QUICK_START_GOOGLE_ADS.md for easiest way to get them)

Q: How long does setup take?
A: ~15 minutes total (5-10 for credentials, 1 for .env, 1-2 for testing)

Q: Is my data private?
A: Yes! Everything runs locally on your machine. Google Ads only sees your 
   API requests (which is normal).

Q: Can I use this without a Google Ads account?
A: You need a Google Ads account to get credentials, but don't need to spend money.
   A free account works fine.

Q: How do I troubleshoot?
A: Check QUICK_START_GOOGLE_ADS.md "Troubleshooting" section or 
   backend/frontend logs (npm run dev output)
